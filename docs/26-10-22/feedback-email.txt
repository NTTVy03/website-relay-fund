Opening text: The current blurb reads:
"Every female student has her own dreams. The Relay Fund was created so that those dreams wouldn't be extinguished by difficult circumstances. We connect the community to provide educational opportunities and motivation for these girls to succeed."
Before suggesting edits, could you tell me whether the site's main audience is English or Vietnamese speakers? That will help me give more useful feedback on the wording and grammar. If it is Vietnamese I trust your ability, and if english I have some very light edits. 
"Who We Are" section: This section is much stronger than the opening blurb. One small suggestion: the word "circumstances" feels a bit vague. Naming the specific challenges these students face, such as financial hardship or limited access to schools, would help readers connect more with the mission.
Application process: It may be worth considering whether linking from a Facebook post to a Google Form is the best way to collect applications, or whether another method would be more reliable or accessible for applicants.
Statistics: The first of the three bubbles ("Each year of additional education can help increase average future income by approximately 10%") is a solid point, but I think a more compelling statistic from the same study would make a stronger first impression. The World Bank reports that Vietnamese university and college graduates "earn substantially more than secondary school graduates (wage premium of 50–90 percent)."
Source: Parajuli, Dilip, Dung Kieu Vo, Jamil Salmi, and Nguyet Thi Anh Tran. 2020. Improving the Performance of Higher Education in Vietnam: Strategic Priorities and Policy Options. Washington, DC: World Bank, p. 4.
Stories section: This section is wonderful. It's engaging and really brings the mission to life. Great work!
Email and domain: For credibility, I'd recommend moving away from a Gmail address to one on the organization's own domain. This could be a .org address or the standard Vietnamese domain for nonprofits and organizations.
